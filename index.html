Licensed entries from the ACMA register, not live status. Channel 5 repeaters are for emergencies only. Distances are straight-line, not driving distance. For UHF CB, RX is the channel you listen on and TX is the channel you transmit on (30 higher). Marine, amateur and aeronautical entries show the frequency the station transmits on. Press the duplex button on your radio to use a repeater.

Data sources

  • ACMA Register of Radiocommunications Licences: source of the station data
  • Leaflet (via cdnjs): map library
  • OpenStreetMap tiles and Nominatim: map tiles and address search
  • Esri World Street Map and World Imagery: map tiles
  • leaflet-rotate (via jsDelivr): map rotation
  • CARTO basemaps: map tiles
  • Photon (Komoot): address search
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API: address search fallback
  • Geolocation API and Device Orientation events: your position and compass, used only in your browser
  • Google Maps URLs: the Directions links
